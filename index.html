Internet Community Library (ICL)

Launched (25 Nov, 2026) by Joseph Willrich Lutalo, also the rightful owner of all the intellectual property currently published here, ICL is the Internet Community Library—a place where netizens can convene to browse and read the best and latest literature from UIC (core Uganda Internet Community). As you go about reading or browsing through this collection (not even all of what we could have presented as of now), think—and fairly so—if it is not right that Uganda, including some of you, dear readers, owes Joseph a salary, a PhD, and his professorship.
